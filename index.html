Eight analytical artifacts built from National Transit Database public data, exercising modern techniques for transit operator benchmarking: DEA frontier estimation, Malmquist productivity decomposition, cost structure analysis, geographic synthesis, safety-efficiency trade-off analysis, worker-exposure rate normalization, and difference-in-differences with synthetic controls. Every artifact is a reusable R + D3 pipeline. Every methodology is documented to survive cross-examination.
